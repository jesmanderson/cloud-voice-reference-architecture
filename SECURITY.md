# Repository Security and Content-Sanitization Policy

This repository must not contain confidential, proprietary, customer,
employee, or production infrastructure information.

Do not commit:

- Employer or customer names
- Internal project names or identifiers
- Employee names or email addresses
- Production phone numbers
- Public or private production IP addresses
- Internal domains, FQDNs, or URLs
- Carrier account or SIP trunk details
- Certificates, private keys, secrets, or tokens
- Exact firewall rules
- Production configuration exports
- Internal screenshots
- Support tickets, incident numbers, or work items
- Unapproved company documents
- Vulnerabilities or known infrastructure weaknesses

Use fictional examples and documentation-reserved values.
